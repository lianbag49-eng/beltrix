Privacy Policy

Pre-launch data-handling framework for counsel review.

Data categories

The service is designed to process account email, residence country, connected exchange and UID, fee/cashback records, payout requests, security logs and operator audit records.

Purpose

Data is used to operate accounts, verify referral relationships, calculate eligible cashback, process payout requests, secure the service and meet applicable operational or legal obligations.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Authentication cookies are HTTP-only. Administrator MFA and audit logging are supported. Production retention and deletion schedules should be finalized before launch.

Pre-launch notice

This is not a finalized jurisdiction-specific privacy notice. Legal review and required contact/entity details must be added before production launch.
