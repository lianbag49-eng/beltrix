Risk Notice

Trading-fee cashback does not reduce the market risk of trading digital assets.

Digital-asset prices can move rapidly and losses can exceed expected trading costs.
Leverage can materially amplify gains and losses.
Exchange availability, fee schedules, referral eligibility and campaigns can change.
Cashback records can be adjusted when the exchange reverses or corrects underlying commission data.
FEELOOP does not provide investment advice or guarantee profitability.
This draft should be reviewed and localized for each launch jurisdiction before production use.
