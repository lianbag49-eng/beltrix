Terms of Service

Pre-launch framework for counsel review before production launch.

Service scope

FEELOOP is designed to display eligible exchange referral-fee cashback records, exchange UID connection status, event information and payout requests. It does not operate an exchange and does not execute trades on behalf of members.

Eligibility

Availability is jurisdiction-dependent. Customer onboarding is not offered in blocked jurisdictions. Exchange-specific eligibility and partner terms may also apply.

Cashback

Cashback is subject to verified partner data, eligibility rules, corrections, reversals and payout controls. No rate should be treated as guaranteed until it is shown as active for the relevant exchange and account.

Pre-launch notice

This text is an operational draft, not final legal advice. Production Terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel in the entity's launch jurisdiction.
