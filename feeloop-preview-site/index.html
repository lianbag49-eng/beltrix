How FEELOOP works

Simple for users. Traceable for operators.

1
Join through a supported partner link
The user remains a customer of the exchange.
2
Connect and verify the exchange UID
Only direct, eligible referral relationships are accepted.
3
FEELOOP syncs eligible fee records
Commission records are deduplicated and written to an auditable ledger.
4
User requests payout
V1 uses manual operator approval and records transfer ID / TXID after payment.
